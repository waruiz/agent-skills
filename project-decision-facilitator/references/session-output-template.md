# Project decision brief template

Use this template when the discussion reaches a natural convergence point. Keep it proportional to the project. Omit sections that truly do not apply.

## Project idea

One or two sentences describing the project in the user's language.

## Invariant

The outcome or principle that must remain true even if implementation details change.

## Intended experience and success evidence

- Intended users, participants, or beneficiaries
- Desired experience or workflow
- Observable evidence that the first useful version succeeds

## Decision chain

List accepted decisions in dependency order.

| ID | Decision | Basis | Why it matters | Downstream effect |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D-001 | ... | Stated / Reasoned / Verified | ... | ... |

## Risks and gates

- Highest-risk unknowns
- Safety, privacy, cost, legal, operational, or quality constraints
- Conditions that gate the first useful release

## Recommended delivery sequence

1. Foundation or prerequisite
2. Smallest meaningful end-to-end validation
3. Highest-risk capability
4. Hardening and observability
5. Acceptance and handoff

Explain dependencies rather than merely listing tasks.

## Open decisions

- Decision still needed
- Why it is not yet resolvable
- Evidence or person needed

## Immediate next action

One concrete action now justified by the accepted decisions.

## Evolution summary

Briefly state what was initially broad, which decisions narrowed the idea, what remains uncertain, and how the project is now positioned to move forward.
